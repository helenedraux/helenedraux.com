# Builds

I like building things: be it digital or manual. Much of my recent digital work was done while employed, so I share here the concepts and what I learned, but not the implementations. 
Here are smaller and more personal builds than the work elsewhere on this site: less polished, more exploratory, and not always pointed at research. 
Loom was a project I carried out to test the balance between deterministic and small local language model to summarise text, and which I have found helpful to log my knowledge as it happens; while our local-first voice assistant was built on my own time to let my children decide what to listen, to get answers to their constant questions, and to introduce them to AI, without using a commercial model.
