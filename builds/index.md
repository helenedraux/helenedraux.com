# Builds

I like building things: to test an idea, to understand how things work, or just because a problem won't leave me alone. Much of that work was done while employed, so I can share the concepts and what I learned, but not the implementations. 
These are smaller and more personal than the work elsewhere on this site: less polished, more exploratory, and not always pointed at research. 
Loom was a project I carried out to test the balance between deterministic and small local language model to summarise text, while our local-first voice assistant was built on my own time to let my children decide what to listen, to get answers to their constant questions, and to introduce them to AI.
