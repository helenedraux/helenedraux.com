I've spent more than a decade building methods for understanding research systems: how knowledge is structured, how it moves, and increasingly, how AI behaves inside it.

This site documents that work — methods, frameworks, tools, and the projects that don't fit neatly into either. Some of it is finished; some is still moving. The emphasis throughout is on how things actually work, not how they should in principle.
