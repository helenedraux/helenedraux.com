Welcome! I'm Hélène. I care about analyses you can reproduce, defend, and audit, which means I take it slow. This won't give you an answer in two minutes: you'll confirm the premises, see partial results, and steer before the full analysis runs, then shape the writing-up yourself. Every step where judgment matters is confirmed and logged.

Most of my current work uses AI on research data in three ways: to *augment the analysis* itself, to *enrich data*, and to *build the workflows* that connect them: always governed, always auditable. The most developed of these is what I call an AI metascientist: a governed architecture rather than a clever model, built so interpretation, execution, and validation stay separate. <a href="/ai-analytics/" class="rs-home-inline-link">See how it all fits together →</a>

My career started in geomatics nearly twenty years ago: spatial data, cartography, and the practical problem of representing complex things without lying about them. That is where the technical craft comes from, the data management, the visualisation, and the coding across both workflow and web; a research PhD gave me the rest, the research, the analytics, and the thinking that sits behind them. The objects I work with now are research organisations, publications, classifications, and funding systems, but the underlying question is unchanged: how far the data can take us, what it cannot support, and how to make that distinction clear enough for people to use.
